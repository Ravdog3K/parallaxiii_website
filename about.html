About

Parallax III builds tools for people who collect, analyse, and report on open-source information.

By combining practitioner experience with best practice guidance, we seek to remove complexity and enhance productivity by developing tools that are powerful, flexible, and intuitive.

Utilising digital forensics, open source investigations, and visual analysis techniques, Parallax III is creating a unified working environment that helps you to move your investigations forward.

Parallax III is led by Tom Rawicz-Szczerbo whose experience in digital forensics, open source investigations, and system design shapes the products we create.
